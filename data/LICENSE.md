# Data licence

The event and source records in `data/` are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

They include or derive from:
- **Wikidata** (CC0): identifiers, coarse dates and coordinates of imported records
- **Wikipedia** (CC BY-SA 4.0): paraphrased facts where Wikipedia is cited
- **OpenStreetMap** (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0): coordinates of present-day places (`modern[].osm`)
- **Public-domain US Government works** (US Army Center of Military History)
- Facts cited from copyrighted works, with short quotations for verification only

Each record lists its sources field by field. The source register is in `data/sources/`.
